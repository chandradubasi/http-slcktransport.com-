Experience, Expertise & Excellence

About SLCK Transport

With over 15 years of industry experience, SLCK delivers reliable solutions across earthmoving, heavy equipment transportation, quarrying, and industrial projects. Our experienced teams, modern machinery, and commitment to safety and quality enable us to deliver every project with excellence.

Trusted Since 2008

Since 2008, SLCK has built a strong and trusted track record across infrastructure, industrial, renewable energy, and government projects throughout India. Our core business includes providing earthmoving machinery on rental basis, supporting a wide range of construction, infrastructure, mining, solar, windmill, and industrial projects.

In addition to our earthmoving equipment rental business, SLCK has established an excellent track record in the transportation industry, providing Trailer Transport and Heavy Equipment Transportation services. We specialize in transporting excavators, heavy machinery, industrial equipment, and other oversized equipment with a strong focus on safe handling, reliable service, on-time delivery, and customer satisfaction.

Earthmoving Equipment Fleet

Our earthmoving equipment fleet includes Hyundai 360, Hyundai 300, Hyundai 215, Tata Hitachi 360, Tata Hitachi 210, Tata Hitachi 200, XCMG 215, and other heavy machinery to meet the diverse requirements of our customers.

SLCK Global Machinery Limited

In 2025, SLCK Global Machinery Limited expanded the group's presence into international markets, with a focus on heavy machinery, earthmoving equipment, machinery transportation, and related services outside India.

Our international operations are built on the same values that have driven our success in India — experience, quality, safety, reliability, timely execution, and customer-focused service.
